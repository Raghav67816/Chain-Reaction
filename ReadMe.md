## Chain Reaction

You work in a data center with your friend and suddenly the AI turns rogue. So, now you must escape the data center by fighting drones, bombers and solving puzzle. Upgrade by finishing the enemies, collecting loot and crafting new gadgets.

It's a lot of work, you need food. But, be careful how much you eat. The more you eat the slower you get. So, eat, blast and make the data center go boom.

## Controls

 - Movement: WASD
 - Jump: W
 - Shoot: Left Click / Space
 - Pause Game: P


## Gameplay

You are in a data center. There are drones around you. And you have..... water balloons. Hit them with water balloons, and watch out for the laser coming out. Once you finish the drone, it drops parts.

Collect the parts, create new gadgets and fight other powerful machine, solve puzzles and escape.
